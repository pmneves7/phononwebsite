The FM chain comparison covered 15 common nonzero-energy q points with maximum
energy difference `1.00e-8 meV` and maximum summed dipole-mode weight difference
`5.55e-16`. The AFM comparison covered seven common nonzero-energy q points,
with differences `2.61e-8 meV` and `1.52e-7`, respectively. The fixtures use
different global spin orientations, so this comparison uses rotationally
invariant total weights. It does not assert equality of raw eigenvectors or of
individual basis vectors inside a degenerate subspace.

The native suite checks:

- Analytic FM and AFM spectra, a doubled magnetic cell, circular handedness,
  and unit one-magnon transverse weight.
- Analytic CoRh₂O₄ nearest-neighbor diamond-lattice spectrum and ground-state
  energy `−2.835 meV` per spin, with the primitive two-site magnetic cell.
- Full Ewald dipole interactions in the pyrochlore tutorial and explicit
  conversion of its kelvin energy values to meV.
- FeI₂ SU(3) with all eight physical modes, and nonzero longitudinal spin
  matrix elements from its relaxed four-site coherent-state configuration.
- A separate exact single-ion SU(3) case with a zero-dipole quadrupolar branch
  at `0.5 meV` and a bright branch at `2.25 meV`.
- A nonreciprocal Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya chain, verifying that native qz = −¼
  has energy `2.4 meV` and +¼ has `3.6 meV` with the chosen polarization.
- Elliptical precession in `:dipole_uncorrected` anisotropy, with analytic
  frequency `sqrt(20) meV` and transverse axis ratio `sqrt(5/4)`.
- Reconstruction of native bare-spin spectral intensities from exported
  site vectors and basis phases, including degenerate-subspace sums.
- JSON round-trip, Unicode labels, shape/index conventions, invalid paths,
  unsupported calculator rejection, reciprocal-basis conversion, and matching
  the viewer's integer repetition limits of 1 through 50.

The original chain tutorials intentionally use a nonconventional tetragonal
cell with the short chain axis first. Both Sunny releases print an informational
message about that choice; it is not a failed validation.

These checks validate spin-dipole visualization, not complete multipolar-state
visualization, finite-temperature dynamics, incommensurate spirals, or entangled
unit modes. Sunny 0.8.x is within the declared compatibility range but was not
executed in this validation run. Future Sunny versions should rerun this suite
because the adapter reads internal local frames.
