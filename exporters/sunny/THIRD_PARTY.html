Sunny source attribution

The example models and local-mode reconstruction follow Sunny’s official examples and src/SpinWaveTheory/DispersionAndIntensities.jl: https://github.com/SunnySuite/Sunny.jl

The SW01 and SW02 examples were originally authored for SpinW by Goran Nilsen, Sandor Toth, and Bjorn Fak, and subsequently ported by Sunny contributors. The compact model scripts here retain their physical parameters while removing plotting and large exploratory computations.
