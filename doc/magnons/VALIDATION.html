Checks include native energies and complex mode observables, analytic chain spectra, momentum signs, precession, ellipticity, and full cross-solver mode covariances. Degenerate subspaces are compared together; arbitrary eigenvector phases are not compared directly.

Sunny examples include CoRh₂O₄, Ewald dipoles, and FeI₂ SU(3). SpinW includes noncollinear kagome and triangular models. Magnopy examples use its native FM and AFM tutorials; they do not test a DFT workflow.

All files pass the browser parser. Debug/production builds and guide links were checked. Browser checks covered branch selection, centered arrows, tip paths, movies, narrow-screen controls, and phonon/exciton regressions. Optional external Playwright checks were not run.

Scope: stable modes in explicit periodic cells. Damping, incommensurate rotating frames, and full multipolar dynamics are not supported. Magnopy may return nonorthogonal modes at exact degeneracies; those are rejected without repair.
